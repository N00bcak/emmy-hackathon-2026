Use this skill for an explicitly requested discovery campaign or its named stage.
It does not start a campaign, background service, workers, sweeps, or hosted calls
by being read. For ordinary bug repair, follow AGENTS.md and run relevant tests;
resume discovery only when the user asks.


Read root AGENTS.md, its challenge documents and [the research guide](../../../docs/research-loop.md).
Resume from NFS START.md, current baseline/strategy, assignment handoff and linked
mechanism notes; verify referenced artifacts. Do not rely on inherited chat alone.

The active session coordinates three bounded research workers where supported.
Keep two coverage responsibilities until all five recorded gaps close; completed
assignments take the next gap. Use the remaining slot for speed, and temporarily
use blocked capacity for useful independent questions. Keep summaries compact.

Before implementation, search experiments and mechanism records with the research
CLI. Address contradictions and semantic overlap, not just exact duplicate IDs.
Choose a question that could materially change the next decision. State its
mechanism, legal IR conditions, expected generated-code effect, alternative
explanation, cheap probe, rough opportunity and budget. Distinguish compile,
assembly, launch and execution costs. Prioritize with credible limits; leave room
for unconventional falsifiable ideas when estimates are uncertain.

Agents may redesign, combine related transformations, pivot, or return a useful
null finding. A bounded sweep can explore internal choices (default at most 16),
not change prescribed launch metadata. Do not exhaust arbitrary parameter grids
or manufacture candidates to fill the GPU. Generalize from IR, never fixture IDs.

Coordinator-owned, independently reviewed generators and fixed tolerance policies
must precede coverage compiler research. Mathematical references must not share
online-softmax/contraction lowering logic. Keep five-gap campaign progress separate
from infrastructure acceptance and from unverified hidden-set behavior.
